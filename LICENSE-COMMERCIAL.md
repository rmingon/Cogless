## When you need it

The open-source licences already allow commercial use, including selling
products, as long as you publish your modified hardware design files and
firmware under the same licences and tell your users where to find them.

You need a commercial licence if you want to do any of the following:

- sell or ship a product based on the Cogless hardware without publishing
  your modified design files;
- ship the Cogless firmware, or firmware derived from it, without publishing
  its source code under the GPL;
- combine the Cogless firmware with proprietary code in a single product;
- obtain terms the open-source licences do not give, such as a warranty,
  support or indemnification.

## What it covers

The commercial licence covers the parts of Cogless owned by the copyright
holder: the hardware design files under `hardware/`, and the firmware files
carrying the Cogless SPDX header.

It does not cover third-party files. The WCH SDK files (`Core/`, `Peripheral/`,
`Startup/`, `Debug/`, and the `system_ch32v00X.*`, `ch32v00X_it.*` and
`ch32v00X_conf.h` files) remain under WCH's own terms.
